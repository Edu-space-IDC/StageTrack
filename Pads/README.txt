StageTracks pad banks

Soft\ contains the generated 50-second stereo WAV loops (44.1 kHz) based on
the pad sound used in the official website preview. Regenerate them with
tools/generate_site_pad_loops.py (requires Python, NumPy and SciPy).

Default\ contains the original Worship MP3 loops that shipped with StageTracks.

Choose either sound bank in the Pads panel. Both banks loop during playback and
use the same pad fader and fade controls.
